Testing checklist

Please check each page below. If something looks wrong, note the page, the row label, your device and browser, and add a screenshot if you can.

On every page

  • Content and layout match the approved design.
  • Nothing overlaps, gets cut off or is hard to read.
  • It looks right on a phone, a tablet and a desktop.
  • It works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.
  • All buttons and links go to the right place.
  • You can use the page with just the keyboard: Tab to move, Enter to select, Esc to close pop-ups.

By component

  • Hero: the banner image or background video shows, “play” buttons open the video, and the background video pauses while a video is open.
  • Icon cards: the icons animate when you hover over them.
  • Modal cards: each card opens its pop-up, the pop-up closes with the X, Esc or by clicking outside it, and videos stop when it closes.
  • Two column: the yellow and maroon versions, with text on the left and on the right, all look correct.
  • Image grid: clicking a photo opens it full screen, the arrows (or arrow keys) move between photos and videos, and videos stop when you move on or close.

The round menu button in the bottom-left corner and the “Row 1 · …” labels above each example are only there to help with testing. They won’t be on the live site.
